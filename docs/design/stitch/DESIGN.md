## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a poised, culturally grounded, and quiet luxury aesthetic designed for the complexities of modern Indian wedding planning. It balances the warmth and joy of multi-day celebrations with the disciplined clarity of enterprise project management.

### Brand Personality & Emotional Impact
- **Regal yet Unpretentious:** Avoids garish gold foil, magenta gradients, and cliché ornamentation in favor of deep botanical forest tones, warm ivory canvases, and refined earth mineral accents.
- **Intergenerational Trust:** The interface bridges tech-fluent couples with traditional parents and extended family elders. Readability, generous tap targets, predictable hierarchies, and high-contrast structural typography form the bedrock of the experience.
- **Calm Amid Complexity:** By framing logistics—such as guest accommodation matrices, vendor payment milestones, and tiered multi-ceremony itineraries—within serene, editorial layouts, the interface replaces wedding anxiety with structured momentum.

### Design Movement
- **Editorial Modernism with Organic Warmth:** Clean grid structures, expansive whitespace, and strict utilitarian legibility meet subtle warm-toned borders, tactile interactive cards, and high-contrast transitional serif titles.

## Colors

The palette draws inspiration from traditional Indian ceremonies, raw silk, and heritage architecture, interpreted with architectural restraint.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#1E3A2F`):** Deep Forest Green serves as the primary anchor for actions, key headers, active navigation, and primary button fills. Its deep variant (`#142820`) is reserved for high-emphasis states, toolbars, and solid dark cards.
- **Secondary (`#C26149`):** Restrained Terracotta is used selectively for urgent actions, critical milestones, countdown badges, and key contextual highlights without creating alert fatigue.
- **Tertiary (`#C59E58`):** Muted Festive Gold brings subtle ceremonial warmth to celebratory accents, VIP/family designations, and ceremonial milestone icons. It is strictly matte, avoiding gloss or metallic skeuomorphism.
- **Neutral Primary (`#232725`):** Charcoal Neutral ensures crisp, high-legibility text on light backdrops without the harshness of pitch black.
- **Neutral Secondary (`#5A625D`):** Subdued slate green-gray for secondary metadata, helper descriptions, and unselected tab labels.
- **Surfaces (`#FBF9F5`, `#F4EFEB`, `#FFFFFF`):** The primary canvas is warm ivory (`#FBF9F5`), while surface containers, nested sections, and cards use layered off-white (`#F4EFEB`) and pure white (`#FFFFFF`) to produce soft, natural depth.
- **Outlines & Dividers (`#E6E0D6`):** Low-contrast organic sand outlines ground cards and list items without imposing rigid grid lines.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy balances ceremonial distinction with rigorous operational legibility.

### Display & Headlines: Playfair Display
Used for ceremony titles (e.g., Sangeet, Mehendi, Anand Karaj), couple names, milestone announcements, and section headers. High-contrast transitional serifs provide warmth, heritage, and distinction. Display levels on desktop scale down for mobile viewports to prevent awkward line breaks on narrow phone screens.

### Body & UI Labels: Inter
Used for checklists, RSVP tables, vendor logistics, budgets, and inputs. Inter is selected for its utilitarian neutral metrics, tall x-height, and superior micro-legibility at small point sizes across mobile viewports.

### Accessibility Rules
- Text blocks must never drop below 13px (`body-sm`), with 15px (`body-md`) serving as standard readable content for older family members.
- Captions and helper text (`label-sm`) are restricted to non-critical metadata.
- All primary text combinations must strictly surpass a 4.5:1 contrast ratio against the warm canvas and surface containers.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout system is mobile-first, designed for one-handed family collaboration, and gracefully extends to multi-pane dashboard setups on desktop.

### Layout Model
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column fluid layout with `1rem` outer margins and `1rem` gutters. Touch-friendly vertical stacking with persistent bottom navigation and action sheets.
- **Tablet (768px – 1024px):** 8-column layout with `1.5rem` outer margins. Accommodates split views (e.g., event schedule list on the left, ceremony guest assignments on the right).
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12-column fixed-max layout with a maximum container width of `1280px` centered on the viewport, flanked by generous `2.5rem` margins and `1.5rem` gutters.

### Spacing Philosophy
Spacing tokens enforce rhythm based on multiples of 4px and 8px:
- `space-xs` (4px) and `space-sm` (8px) govern tight component-level pairings like icon-to-label gaps and badge insets.
- `space-md` (16px) establishes standard internal card padding and form field stack spacing.
- `space-lg` (24px) separates card groups and segmented itinerary items.
- `space-xl` (32px) and `space-2xl` (48px) define significant structural boundaries between ceremony stages and dashboard sections.

## Elevation & Depth

To avoid an over-processed, synthetic tech appearance, this system avoids harsh black drop shadows and blurry neon glassmorphism. It uses soft, warm-tinted organic depth based on physical paper and linen surfaces.

### Surface Hierarchy
1. **Canvas (Base Level):** `#FBF9F5` (Warm Ivory) – Grounds the entire viewport with a soft, non-reflective base.
2. **Surface Low:** `#F4EFEB` (Muted Sand) – Used for subtle grouped sections, inactive tabs, and segmented controls.
3. **Surface Card (Level 1):** `#FFFFFF` (Pure White) with a `1px` border of `#E6E0D6` (Organic Sand) and an ambient, low-contrast shadow: `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(30, 58, 47, 0.04), 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(30, 58, 47, 0.02)`.
4. **Surface Floating / Sheet (Level 2):** `#FFFFFF` with an expanded ambient shadow: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(30, 58, 47, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(30, 58, 47, 0.04)`. Used for bottom modal sheets, ceremony filter panels, and dropdown menus.
5. **Surface Overlay / Modal (Level 3):** `#FFFFFF` overlaid on a tinted scrim: `rgba(20, 40, 32, 0.45)`.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a roundedness factor of `2`, utilizing proportional radii that deliver welcoming, organic softness without turning into cartoonish bubbles.

### Geometry Hierarchy
- **Standard UI Elements (`0.5rem` / 8px):** Form inputs, dropdown selectors, checkboxes, data table cells, and nested items.
- **Interactive Surfaces & Cards (`1rem` / 16px):** Standard event cards, itinerary trackers, vendor overview tiles, and accommodation summaries.
- **Large Hero Containers & Bottom Sheets (`1.5rem` / 24px):** Ceremony highlight cards, mobile drawer top corners, and primary modal wrappers.
- **Full Pill (`9999px`):** Floating action buttons (FABs), filter chips, ceremony timeline tags (e.g., "Haldi", "Pheras"), and status pills.

## Components

Components are engineered for intergenerational usability, supporting rapid touch manipulation on smartphones and organized operational oversight on laptops.

### Buttons
- **Touch Target:** Strict minimum of 48px height across all interactive states on mobile devices.
- **Primary Button:** Background `#1E3A2F`, text `#FFFFFF`, border-radius 8px (`rounded-md`) or full pill when floating. In hover/active states, shifts to `#142820`.
- **Secondary Button:** Surface `#F4EFEB` with a 1px border in `#E6E0D6` and text in `#1E3A2F`. Hover state darkens the surface subtly to `#EAE2D8`.
- **Accent/Destructive:** Terracotta `#C26149` filled or outlined for critical budget cutoffs and RSVP cancellations.

### Chips & Ceremony Tags
- **Event Chips:** Full pill radius (`rounded-full`), height 36px, internal horizontal padding 16px.
- **Selected State:** Background `#1E3A2F` with white typography.
- **Unselected State:** Background `#FFFFFF` with a 1px border in `#E6E0D6` and neutral charcoal text (`#232725`).
- **Ceremony Indicators:** Dedicated soft tints with solid indicator dots (e.g., Gold `#C59E58` for Muhurtham/Pheras, Terracotta `#C26149` for Sangeet).

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- **Input Fields:** Minimum height 48px, background `#FFFFFF`, border 1px `#E6E0D6`, text `#232725` (`body-md`).
- **Focus State:** 1.5px border in `#1E3A2F` with a soft 3px outer glow ring of `rgba(30, 58, 47, 0.12)`. No harsh browser default outlines.
- **Floating Labels / Clear Labels:** Always explicitly placed above the field in `label-md` charcoal for unambiguous clarity among family members.

### Checkboxes & Multi-Party Approvals
- **Hit Area:** 44px outer invisible hit area around a 20px physical checkbox with 6px rounded corners.
- **Checked State:** Fill `#1E3A2F` with a crisp white checkmark icon.
- **Approval Badges:** Dedicated dual-state indicators displaying "Bride's Side Approved" or "Groom's Side Approved" using pill badges.

### Cards & Event Lists
- **Ceremony Itinerary Cards:** White surface (`#FFFFFF`) with a 16px corner radius (`rounded-lg`), sand border (`#E6E0D6`), and left-side accent trim corresponding to the ceremony color code. Internal padding strictly set to `1.5rem` (`space-lg`).
- **Guest / Rooming Allocation Cards:** Compact vertical stacks with clear divider lines (`#E6E0D6`), pairing full family member names in `title-md` with dietary, transport, and rooming tags below in `label-sm`.

### Multi-Event Family Switcher (Specialized Component)
- A persistent, horizontally scrolling top bar housing pill chips for each ceremony (e.g., *All, Mehendi, Sangeet, Wedding, Reception*), providing rapid context switching for parents and planners without buried dropdown menus.
